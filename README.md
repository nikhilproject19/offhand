# Offhand

A matchmaking prototype with no AI and no second takes: one live photo, random questions answered on a clock, and no pasting anywhere.

Conversations are live and one at a time. Each opens with a random question you both answer, then you take turns on a 30-second clock. The other person's message shows once and disappears when you reply, leaving the app ends the conversation, and there's no history to scroll back to.

- **Remember last time:** before you talk to someone again, you both answer two quick questions about your last conversation, and each of you sees how much the other remembered.

- **Go live:** you're available for 20 minutes, only while the app is open, and matches who are here can knock on you.
- **Likes need words:** you have 15 seconds to say why you like an answer. No words, no like.
- **Watch it typed:** every typed answer can be replayed as it was typed, pauses and corrections included.
- **Full focus record:** profiles show how often someone stayed until the end of a live conversation.
- **Dealt questions:** mid-conversation, the other person can deal you a surprise question from the bank.

Live demo: https://nikhilproject19.github.io/offhand/

This is a single-page prototype. Everything a visitor enters stays in their own browser, and the other profiles are examples with scripted replies.

## Run it on your computer

```
node server.js
```

Then open http://localhost:4173.
